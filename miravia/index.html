Miravia (Alibaba) · Campaigns & Social · Web Design

Miravia

Alibaba's European e-commerce platform needed campaign assets that could serve multiple global brand partners simultaneously, each with their own visual identity, brand standards, and commercial objectives, while still feeling cohesive within the Miravia platform itself.

I designed homepage banners, product graphics, landing page assets, PDPs, catalog design, and advertising creatives for Apple, Samsung, Nvidia, Lego, P&G, Maybelline, NYX, Colgate, and Motorola.

Every asset had to meet the standards of a global brand while performing commercially within a fast-moving e-commerce environment. Scale, precision, and speed, all at once.

Campaigns

UX / UI

Designing for an e-commerce platform at Alibaba's scale meant thinking beyond aesthetics.

Every screen, every layout, every product page had a commercial objective behind it and a real user on the other side of it. The design had to be intuitive enough to disappear and distinctive enough to reinforce the brand at the same time.

I worked across the full digital product ecosystem. App interface design, web UI, product detail pages, and catalogue design for global brand partners including Nintendo and Lego.

Each partner had their own visual standards and each asset had to meet those standards while living coherently within the Miravia platform.

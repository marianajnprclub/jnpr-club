Cookie policy

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser so it can remember information between visits.

Cookies on this site

This site only uses analytics cookies from Google Analytics 4, and only if you accept them in the notice. If you decline, nothing from Google Analytics is loaded. There are no advertising cookies and nothing is used to show you ads.

What I use the data for

To see which pages and projects get visited, which buttons are used and how to improve the site. The data is anonymous and aggregated; I do not sell it or share it with anyone else.

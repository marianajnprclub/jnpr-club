The anti-agency* built from scratch

JNPR® started as a flower shop during the pandemic. A first act of creative ownership, built from scratch, in uncertain times but it didn't stay that way for long…

What it became is a one-woman creative practice built on a simple belief: the best brand work happens when strategy and instinct are treated as equals.

Founded by Creative Director Mariana Ortega, JNPR® works with brands, startups, founders, and studios who want a visual world that is genuinely their own. Not templated. Not safe. Not forgettable.

Every project is a direct collaboration and nothing gets lost in translation between the person with the vision and the person executing it.
